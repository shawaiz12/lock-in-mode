# 0035 – XP and levels, in the background

**Date:** 2026-11-26
**Status:** Accepted

## Context
ADR 0034 put real progress first: the weekly ring, the weekly streak and new bests. The
brief's XP and levels (3.5) come next. The plan is `lock-in-mode/xp-levels-plan.md`, and
the retention notes (`lock-in-mode/retention-knowledge.md`) set the limits:
- XP rewards **turning up**, not being strong, so a beginner and an advanced lifter level
  at the same speed;
- it must never be **the headline**: new bests and the kept week come first;
- it must never push **extra workouts** (people overtrain or quit) or **farming** (half a
  set, then finish);
- surprises keep people coming back better than fixed rewards, but only small ones.

## Decisions

### 1. What earns XP
| Source | XP | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Workout | up to 100 | 100 × sets done ÷ sets planned (capped at 1). No ticked set, no XP |
| Week kept | 150 | once per local week, when it reaches 2 trained workouts (ADR 0034) |
| New bests | 25 each | up to 3 per workout (the same bests the finish screen shows) |
| Welcome back | 100 | the first trained workout after a comeback (ADR 0021) |
| Locked-in bonus | = the workout's XP | about 1 workout in 8, never two in a row |

- **The week's limit: days a week + 1.** Past that, a workout earns no workout XP (no
  bests, comeback or bonus either) and the finish screen says "Rest counts too: you've done
  your week." Training more than planned is fine; being paid for it isn't.
- **Scaled by sets done**, so a half-done workout gets half: finishing early is never the
  smart move, and a short day still counts.
- **The bonus** is decided by the workout's id (sha256, 1 in 8), not a random number at
  finish time: sending the same finish twice can't roll twice, and tests can pin it.

### 2. Levels
Level *n* starts at 50 × (*n*−1) × *n* XP up to level 10 (100, 300, 600, … 4,500), then
every level is 1,000 more. With about 3 workouts and a kept week a week (≈450 XP), level 2
comes in the first week, level 5 in under 3 weeks, level 10 in about 10 weeks, then a level
every 2–3 weeks: always the next one in sight, never a wall.

### 3. Stored as rows, awarded once
- `xp_events`: user, source, amount, and the workout (or the week's Monday for the week
  award). Unique per (workout, source) and per (user, week), inserted with
  `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`: a retried finish, a late offline sync or the backfill can't
  pay twice. The total is a `SUM`. CHECKs keep the week rows and the workout rows apart.
- Awarded **when a workout closes**, inside the same transaction as the finish (online or
  synced later). The week is the phone's local week (ADR 0034's time zone).
- `GET /me/xp` → `{total, level, xp_into, xp_needed}`. `SessionResult.xp` → what this
  workout added (items, total gained, bonus, capped, level before and after).
- A `level_up` event (level) for the report; deleted with the account; in the export.

### 4. The finish screen: the reveal (his choice: animated now)
In order, one step every 0.45 s: new bests (confetti) → "This week counts" → the XP card
(`+N XP` counting up, then its lines) or the rest line → the level bar filling. A level-up
says "Lock In level N!" with confetti and one success buzz. With **Reduce motion** on,
everything shows at once, no count-up, no confetti. Plain React Native `Animated` on the
native driver; the confetti is 16 small squares (his choice: built in, no package).

Home shows "Lock In level 7 · 320 / 1,000 XP" with a bar, under the weekly ring. A workout
finished offline says its XP lands when it syncs.

### 5. Workouts before XP existed
`python -m lock_in_mode_api.xp_backfill` gives every trained workout without XP its XP,
oldest first, by the same rules, without bonus rolls or `level_up` events (nobody sees a
surprise for last month). CI runs it after the seed on each deploy; once done it adds
nothing. A workout finished on the old version during a deploy gets its XP at the next one.

### 6. Report
"Lock In level-ups" in the counts; how many people reached levels 2, 5, 10, 20+; and the
bonus rate ("about 1 in 8" is the aim, so a broken roll shows up).

## Found while building
- New bests moved to `services/bests.py` (XP needs them too) and now compare only against
  **earlier** workouts: a synced old workout no longer counts a later one's sets.
- The finish screen was drawn twice (Home stays mounted under the player and drew its own
  copy of the result), so the level-up buzz fired twice. Home's copy now only shows when
  Home is the screen in front (`useIsFocused`).
- React's `set-state-in-effect` lint: the count-up keeps its own animated value and renders
  the final number directly when motion is reduced.
- Tests switch the bonus roll off (an autouse fixture) except where it's being tested.

## Consequences
- DB: new table `xp_events` (3 indexes, 3 CHECKs). Autogenerated by him after extracting
  the zip. Nothing existing changes.
- API: `GET /me/xp`; `SessionResult.xp`; event `level_up`; export gains `xp`.
- CI: the deploy step also runs the XP backfill.
- App: `ui/reveal.tsx` (steps, fade, count-up, fill bar, confetti, buzz),
  `features/xp/words.ts`, the finish screen's reveal, Home's level card; `expo-haptics` is
  already in the project, so no new package.
